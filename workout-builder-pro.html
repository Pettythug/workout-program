GUIDE & TUTORIALS

🔄 How to Swap Exercises

On the PLAN screen, each exercise card has a variation selector (e.g., Standard, Single Arm, Alternating). Use this to swap variations dynamically. To swap with a custom or different exercise, click the dropdown inside the card header, select the new exercise, and click the swap icon to save it in your plan.

💪 How to Log Sets

Tap the clock icon on any exercise card to slide open its history drawer and review your last 10 log entries. Click + LOG to navigate to the logging screen, enter your reps/weight, and hit Save Set. Your best sets are automatically calculated and updated instantly.

👥 Managing Your Roster

Click the ⚙️ settings gear in the top right header to open Settings. Here you can add new teammates to your roster, check or uncheck names to toggle their visibility, and assign a primary Device Owner to manage the session.
